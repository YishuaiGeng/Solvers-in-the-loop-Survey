# Figure credits

The seven PNG illustrations in `figures/` are display derivatives of the survey's existing artwork, prepared by the survey authors. They introduce the overview, five solver roles and assurance boundaries. They were resized proportionally to a maximum dimension of 2,000 pixels, converted to RGB, and saved without source metadata. Content and labels were not redrawn for this release.

The [snapshot manifest](../data/snapshot.json) records each source image's SHA-256 and the display transformation. The [release manifest](../release-manifest.json) fingerprints the published PNGs. Figure numbering is omitted so links remain stable across manuscript layout changes.
