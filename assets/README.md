# Figure credits

The eight PNG illustrations in `figures/` are display derivatives of the survey's existing artwork. They introduce the overview, solver development tree, five solver roles and assurance boundaries. The development tree was rendered from the existing standalone figure PDF at a maximum dimension of 3,200 pixels so its labels remain readable when opened at full resolution. The other illustrations were resized proportionally to a maximum dimension of 2,000 pixels. All PNGs use RGB and omit source metadata; content and labels were not redrawn.

The [snapshot manifest](../data/snapshot.json) records each source asset's SHA-256 and the display transformation. The [release manifest](../release-manifest.json) fingerprints the published PNGs. Figure numbering is omitted so links remain stable across manuscript layout changes.

The development tree arranges selected foundations, tools and LLM configurations by family and cited-report year. Institutional and product marks embedded in the existing figure identify their respective organizations or tools; their rights remain with their owners, and their appearance does not imply endorsement.
